The short version: this site does not collect anything about you.

**No cookies.** The site sets none.

**No analytics.** There is no tracking script, no pixel, no third-party
measurement of any kind running today. If that changes, this page changes with it.

**No accounts, no forms.** There is nothing to sign up for and nowhere to enter
personal information, so none is stored.

**Server logs.** The site is static files served by a hosting provider. Like
essentially all web hosts, that provider records standard request information —
including IP addresses — for delivery and abuse prevention. That is the host's
processing, not something this site controls or has access to.

**Links out.** When you follow a link to a retailer, you are on their site under
their privacy policy, not this one. If a link is an affiliate link, the retailer
may record that the visit came from here. See the [affiliate disclosure]({{< relref "/disclosure" >}}).
