Most shoe shops stop at size 12 or 13, or they list a big size and turn out to
be sold out of it. Sasquatch Index exists to answer one question for people with
big feet: **where can I get this shoe, in my size, right now.**

## What the site does

- It lists men's shoes in **size 13 and up**, with width where the retailer
  gives it.
- Each listing shows a picture and a price, and the whole card is a link to the
  retailer that has the shoe.
- It sells nothing. There is no cart, no account and no mailing list.

## Where the listings come from

- Every listing comes from a **product feed** that a retailer publishes through
  an affiliate network. Nothing is copied from shop websites by hand and nothing
  is scraped.
- A size is listed only when the retailer's feed showed it **in stock and for
  sale** at the last daily check. A size that is missing from the feed is not
  listed.
- If a retailer's feed stops updating for **30 days**, that retailer's shoes are
  taken off the site. They come back by themselves once the feed updates again.
  Showing an empty page is better than sending you to a sold-out one.

## What the site does not do

- No scores, rankings or "top picks". The site has not worn these shoes, so it
  does not pretend to judge them.
- No reviews written in anyone's voice, and no claims that anyone tried a shoe.
- Nothing is placed higher because a retailer pays more. See the
  [affiliate disclosure](/disclosure/).

## The reference guides

The [sizing guide](/shoes/sizing/) and the [width guide](/shoes/widths/) are
factual and free to use. They cover US, UK and EU sizes past 13 and what width
letters such as 2E and 4E mean.
